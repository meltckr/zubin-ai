How to take advantage of new model releases

A simple playbook for testing a new AI model against your real work instead of the hype.

Every release comes with charts. Your work isn't a chart. Here's how to find out in an hour whether a new model actually helps you.

1. Keep a personal test set

Save 5 to 10 real tasks you care about with a known good answer: a brief you wrote, a bug you fixed, an email you'd send. This is your benchmark.

2. Run old vs new side by side

Same brief, same inputs, both models. Compare results blind if you can. Note speed and cost too.

3. Look for new abilities, not just better scores

Longer context, better tool use, cheaper speed tiers, or a new effort setting can unlock jobs that weren't worth automating before.

4. Switch one routine at a time

Move a single low-risk routine to the new model for a week. Keep the old one ready to switch back.

5. Re-check your briefs

Newer models often follow instructions more literally. Lines you added to work around an old model's quirks may now cause problems. Trim them.

Model names, prices, and limits change constantly. Confirm details in the provider's current docs before you rely on them.
