Start here

An agent is an AI that can take steps for you: read your files, use your apps, and come back with finished work. Here's the shortest path from curious to useful.

Your first week

  1. Day 1: Set up one agent for one job.
  2. Day 2: Write a brief it can follow.
  3. Day 3: Decide what needs your yes.
  4. Day 4: Connect one app, read-only.
  5. Day 5: Put it on a schedule.

Three beliefs that save you time

  • Specific beats smart. A clear brief beats a better model.
  • Drafts are free; sends are forever. Keep the send button.
  • One job done every day beats ten jobs done once.
