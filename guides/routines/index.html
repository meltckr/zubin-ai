Routines and schedules: make an agent show up on time

Turn a one-off task into a daily or weekly routine without it going off the rails.

A routine is just a brief plus a clock. The hard part isn't the clock; it's making the output safe to receive while you're not watching.

Start with a report, not an action

Your first routine should only read and summarize: a morning digest, a weekly metrics roundup, a "what changed" check on a web page. Reports can be wrong without hurting anyone.

Pick times you'll actually read

Schedule it 15 minutes before you normally look. A 6:45a report you read at 7:00 builds the habit; a 3:00a report gets buried.

Make silence meaningful

Tell it: "If nothing changed, say so in one line." A routine that always writes an essay trains you to skip it.

Review weekly

Once a week, skim the last five runs. Kill routines you stopped reading. Fewer, sharper routines beat a noisy pile.
