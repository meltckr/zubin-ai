When your agent goes wrong: a calm debugging checklist

Stalled, looping, confidently wrong, or did something you didn't ask for. What to check first.

  1. Read what it actually did, not what it said it did. Check the file, the sent folder, the log.
  2. Find the missing instruction. Most mistakes trace to a gap in the brief.
  3. Shrink the task. Split one big ask into three small ones.
  4. Check access. "I couldn't find it" often means a connector expired.
  5. Start a fresh session when it starts looping. Long conversations drift.
  6. Tighten the gate. If it did something irreversible, that action goes behind approval today.
